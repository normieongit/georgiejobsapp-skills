# GeorgieJobsApp Skills

GeorgieJobsApp is building the Life Operating System for Self-Employed People. A Blue Collar Technology Platform.

I'm open sourcing the GeorgieJobsApp workflows as skills, for self-employed people, independent workers, and 1–3 person teams.

Quiet operations for real workers and families: turn messages into booked jobs with clear scope, finish jobs, get paid, and keep life organized.

Self-employed trades pros and 1–3 person teams are stuck juggling DMs, random invoices, and missed follow-ups. Enterprise CRMs are overkill. Job boards feel like black holes. These skills do that job instead.

## The same files every time

Every skill reads the same two files:

- `jobs.md` — every booked job: the customer, the clear scope, the date, the status, and the record that goes with it — receipts, notes, and add-ons
- `customers.md` — every customer and the jobs you've done for them

That is how the records stay findable, instead of buried in a thread.

## What's inside

### Start here

1) start-here, reads your messages, past jobs, and family calendar, writes the jobs and customers files, and tells you which skill to use next

### Get booked

2) dm-to-booked-job — a DM, text, or message turned into a booked job with clear scope

3) family-calendar-guard — respect life and work, so a job never gets booked over family

### Do the job

4) find-my-job-record — receipts, notes, and add-ons for any job, findable when you need them

### Get paid

5) get-paid — job done to invoice to paid, with the follow-up if it doesn't land

### Bring them back

6) follow-up-nudge — follow-ups and reminders nudged on time, without micromanaging you or the customer

## Works in your agent on day one

The skills work in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and OpenClaw on day one.

## MCP

An MCP ships with it, so an agent can use GeorgieJobsApp directly:

- Create a booked job from a message, with clear scope
- Find a job record — a receipt, a note, an add-on
- Check a new booking against the family calendar before it lands
- Send a follow-up nudge

## Free vs paid

The free skills do the work once.

The paid GeorgieJobsApp does it every week, with your jobs, customers, and family calendar kept together, so the follow-ups and records are there the next time you need them. Done-for-you setup and team plans for 1–3 person teams are the next step up.
